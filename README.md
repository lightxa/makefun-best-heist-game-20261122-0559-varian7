THE GILDED GALLERY: a night heist in an art museum. Cross three rooms, cut THE GILDED SUN from its frame, and slip out the EXIT past docents, cameras, and lasers. Dodge patrolling docents' vision cones and sweeping wall cameras, thread the laser grid, spend your coin tosses and smoke puffs wisely, and watch the alarm meter.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory game
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Move with WASD or the arrow keys, click to toss a coin that lures the nearest guard, press Q for a smoke puff. Good luck, and don't trip the alarm.
